# Data licence

The files under `results/rows/` are generations produced by `CohereLabs/tiny-aya-l2-thinker`
(revision `e9feb287…`) and its trimmed derivative, in response to prompts from MGSM
(Shi et al. 2022, CC-BY-SA 4.0) and the Aya Collection (Cohere For AI, Apache 2.0).
The model is released under CC-BY-NC 4.0 for research use only; its outputs are provided
here under the same terms (CC-BY-NC 4.0, research only, attribution to Cohere Labs).

`keep/l2thinker_trim_keep.json` is a list of token ids and statistics; it contains no
model weights. Model weights and GGUF files are not redistributed.
